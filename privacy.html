Canvassed · Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains what Canvassed, the art history app, keeps about you, why, who else handles it, and what you can ask for. It is written to follow UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

1Who is responsible

Canvassed is made by Jorge Hernandez, in the United Kingdom, who decides how your data is used (the "controller"). For anything about your data, write to canvassed.app@gmail.com.

2What we keep, and why

You can use Canvassed without an account. Until you sign in, your progress stays on your phone and nothing about you is sent to us.

When you make an account, we keep:

  • Your account: your email address, your password (stored scrambled by our database provider, never readable by us), the username you choose, your avatar colour, the country you choose to show (optional), and whether friends and rankings may see your level. Why: to run your account and the app you signed up for (contract).
  • Your learning: your quiz answers and times, XP, the days you played (your streak), the paintings due for review, your favourites, which paintings you looked at and for how long, and when you used the app. Why: to keep your progress on any phone and show you your stats (contract).
  • Friends and duels: who you are friends with and who you invited, your duels and answers, the members you blocked, and reports you made. Friends see your username and your XP for the week, and also your level, knowledge tier and strongest movement unless you switch that off in Settings. Other members see your username; your weekly XP appears in rankings and leagues only if that same switch is on. Why: to run the social features (contract), and to keep members safe (legitimate interest).
  • Sign-in safety: when you sign in with a username or ask for a reset code, we note what was typed, whether it worked and the network address it came from, and delete it after one day. Wrong invite codes are noted for one day too. Our database provider also keeps short-lived technical logs of sign-ins and requests, including network addresses. Why: to stop people guessing passwords or sending floods of emails (legitimate interest).
  • Feedback (test versions only): the message you send, a screenshot if you choose to attach one, the screen you were on, the app version and build, your phone's operating system version and model. Why: to fix problems you report (legitimate interest).
  • Crash and performance reports (everyone, with or without an account): when the app crashes or hits an error, and how long it takes to start, together with the app version, your phone's model and operating system, and an anonymous number for this installation of the app. They contain no name, email or username, and the number changes if you reinstall the app. Why: to find and fix crashes and slow starts (legitimate interest).
  • The painting of the day and streak reminder notifications are set up on your phone, with your permission, and can be switched off in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Push notifications (members only, with your permission): your phone's push address (a code Expo gives the app for this installation), its type (Android or iPhone), the app's language and which kinds you want (duels, friends). They tell you when a friend challenges you, when a duel ends, and about friend requests. Why: to tell you about things you take part in (contract). Switch them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

We do not show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, read your location or contacts, or sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

3Who else handles it

We use a few providers to run the app. They process data for us under contract and may not use it for anything else.

  • Supabase: our database, file storage and server functions, hosted in London, United Kingdom.
  • Brevo: sends our emails, such as password reset codes.
  • Expo: delivers app updates and push notifications, and receives the crash and performance reports; Expo sees your network address and phone type.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (Push Notification service): carry push notifications to Android phones and iPhones; they see the notification's text and your phone's push address.
  • GitHub: keeps our weekly database backups, locked with a passphrase only we hold, so GitHub cannot read them.
  • Google Play and Apple: deliver the app to your phone under their own privacy policies.
  • Museum image services: when you zoom into a painting, the large image comes straight from the museum that owns it (The Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Art Institute of Chicago or the Rijksmuseum), whose server sees your network address like any website would.

Some of these providers may handle data outside the UK. When they do, they use the protections UK law requires, such as UK adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

4How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything linked to it: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your profile, progress, friends, duels and feedback straight away.
  • Reports you made about other members are kept so that we can act on them, with your name removed.
  • Sign-in safety notes and wrong invite codes: one day.
  • Your phone's push address: until you sign out, delete your account, or uninstall the app (it is removed the next time a notification cannot be delivered).
  • Feedback: up to 12 months, or until you delete your account.
  • Crash and performance reports: deleted by Expo after 90 days.
  • Backups of the database: kept for up to 90 days, then deleted.

5Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it in a particular way. Write to canvassed.app@gmail.com from the email address of your account; we answer within one month. You can delete your account yourself at any time: see Deleting your account.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

6Age

Canvassed is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please do not make an account. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete it.

7The guide

"Ask about this painting" currently shows answers we prepared and checked in advance; your questions are not sent anywhere. If typed questions to an AI model are added later, this policy will be updated first to say which provider handles them.

8Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
