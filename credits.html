Every painting in Canvassed comes from a museum that allows its images to be used freely: 312 paintings from 4 museums, listed here with the museum's own credit line and licence. Each title links to the museum's page for the painting.

The wall labels, stories and painter biographies in the app are our own writing, checked against the museums’ records; they are not the museums’ texts.

The English narration uses the synthetic Kokoro voice (Apache 2.0 licence); the Spanish narration uses Microsoft Azure’s synthetic voice Arabella.

If you hold rights in an image and want it taken down, write to canvassed.app@gmail.com with the painting's title; we remove it within 7 days while we check.
