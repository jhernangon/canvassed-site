Deleting your account

Last updated 19 November 2026

You can delete your Canvassed account, and everything linked to it, at any time.

1In the app

  1. Open Canvassed and tap the settings button on the Home screen.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out.

2Without the app

Write to canvassed.app@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 30 days and write back when it is done.

3What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password, username, avatar colour and country.
  • Your learning: quiz answers, XP, streak, review cards, favourites, viewing history and session times.
  • Friends, invites, duels and your duel answers, blocks, and the feedback you sent with its screenshots.

4What is kept

  • Reports you made about other members are kept so that we can act on them, with your name removed.
  • Database backups are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted, so your data may remain in a backup until then. Backups are never used to bring an account back.

Progress you made before signing in is stored on your phone only; uninstalling the app removes it.

5Deleting some of your data and keeping your account

### In the app, straight away

  • Favourites: tap the heart on a painting or painter again to remove it.
  • Friends: open a friend in the You tab and tap Remove friend. Block also ends any duel between you.
  • Rankings and what friends see: in Settings, switch off Show me in rankings and to friends.
  • Country and avatar colour: change or clear them on your profile.

### By email

For anything else, write to canvassed.app@gmail.com from the email address of your account, saying what you want removed. For example:

  • your quiz answers, XP and streak,
  • the paintings due for review,
  • your viewing history and session times,
  • your finished duels,
  • the feedback you sent and its screenshots.

We delete what you name within 30 days and write back when it is done. Your account, and anything you did not name, stays as it is. Deleting your progress resets your level and streak.

What is kept is the same as for a deleted account: reports you made about other members (with your name removed), and database backups for up to 90 days.
